# Unknown black boxes at the cut-off

See `BLACK_BOX_RESEARCH.md` for the expanded evidence audit. “Partially narrowed” does not mean converted into an observed fact.

These are not missing values to be guessed. They are possible contingency variables for later sensitivity runs.

1. **Merz–Frei operating agreement — partially narrowed.** High policy similarity, accumulated operational trust and Frei's explicit faction-role autonomy are observed; the private division of initiative, vetoes and reporting lines is not.
2. **Warken's effective access — partially narrowed.** Prior parliamentary management, a negotiated health compromise, strong direct mandate and Baden-Württemberg party bridges are observed. Whether ministers and Länder accept her as an autonomous broker had not yet been observed.
3. **Hoppermann's mandate beyond transition — partially narrowed.** Merz sponsored two promotions, but she also has an older Röttgen/CDA/Frauen-Union/Hamburg network. Duration, staffing latitude and independent political mandate were not public.
4. **The first parliamentary manager vacancy.** Bilger's move removed the operational centre of faction scheduling and discipline. A later successor must not be backfilled into the July snapshot.
5. **The sport/volunteering state-minister vacancy.** Schenderlein's removal was known; the successor and the final East-German compensation package were not.
6. **Personal loyalty after the reshuffle.** Acceptance of an office, a public endorsement, or attendance at a press conference does not prove personal loyalty.
7. **Fritz Güntzler episode — substantially narrowed.** Multiple reports and his public explanation identify lack of transport expertise as the reason for declining after initially accepting. Additional private considerations and the exact communication chain remain unknown.
8. **State-premier coordination — no cutoff-date bloc evidence found.** Similar criticism from several Länder does not establish a coordinated anti-Merz bloc. Later joint action may not be back-projected.
9. **Wüst's future federal option.** Structural option value is visible; intention, timing and willingness to incur conflict are not.
10. **Söder's future federal option.** CSU institutional leverage is visible; any private candidacy plan is not.
11. **Election-contingent authority.** Berlin, Saxony-Anhalt and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania were approaching September elections. Results and their downstream effects are outside the environment.
12. **Spahn's remaining network — partially narrowed.** Loss of faction office is observed; Frei publicly described a close relationship and possible future leadership role, so total exclusion is unsupported. The mobilisable strength of networks in NRW, the Bundestag and the party remains unmeasured.
