## Interpretation discipline

`OBSERVED_TEXT` means the rule is directly express in the constitutional text.
`ROUTE_ONLY` means the GG requires another organ or procedure but the detailed
procedure belongs to another source. `NOT_COMPILED` means the article was read
but no executable predicate has yet been added.
