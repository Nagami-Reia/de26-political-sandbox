# DE26 Political Sandbox

A political simulation sandbox for modeling institutions, political actors, crises, coalition dynamics, and branching scenarios inside a small controlled world that frequently refuses to remain under control.

## About

DE26 Political Sandbox is an experimental political simulation project built around interacting institutions, political actors, decision-making processes, and evolving crisis scenarios.

Rather than producing a single predetermined outcome, the sandbox allows political events to develop through changing conditions, institutional constraints, actor behavior, and conditional branching.

The project currently focuses primarily on contemporary German political scenarios, including:

- institutional decision-making
- coalition and party dynamics
- political crises
- individual political actors
- constitutional and procedural constraints
- conditional scenario development
- alternative and counterfactual pathways

The objective is to create a small political world, establish its rules, introduce several apparently rational actors, and observe how long it takes before everything becomes considerably less rational.

## Current Status

This repository contains the current working prototype of the simulation system, scenario files, examples, and supporting material.

The project is under active development and the structure may change substantially.
